Live diagnostic panel — Europe
Click the map to load a single point, or pan/zoom then Scan visible area to color the whole view by overall risk at the currently selected time. Each scan is one batched API call per grid, not one call per cell — still, larger grids cost more of your daily Open-Meteo quota. Cell color is coarser than the underlying weather models (~1–2 km temp/wind centrally in Europe, ~11 km CAMS air quality) — treat it as indicative, not a point forecast.

Data: temperature, humidity, wind and CAPE from Open-Meteo (national weather services, includes past observations and forecast); air quality, UV and pollen from Open-Meteo Air Quality API (CAMS-Europe, Copernicus). Heat stress is an estimated outdoor WBGT (Australian simplified formula) — it omits direct solar radiation load, so on cloudless days true heat stress runs higher than shown. This is a screening tool, not medical advice; individual heat tolerance, acclimatisation, hydration and health conditions all shift real risk. Map tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors.
